Chocolate Fudge

Description

Chocolate fudge is a rich and delicious chocolate dessert and very famous all around the world.

It is made with chocolate, butter, sugar, eggs, and flour.

Ingredients

  • Chocolate
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Flour

Steps

  1. Melt the chocolate and butter.
  2. Mix in the sugar and eggs.
  3. Add the flour and stir well.
  4. Pour the mixture into a baking pan.
  5. Bake until done.
